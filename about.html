About Jason

A practitioner, not just a presenter.

Jason Staten is a leadership coach, speaker, and cultural architect with over a decade of experience developing leaders across public utilities, nonprofits, engineering firms, and faith-based organizations.

He has reached 30,000+ leaders in a single arena, and his work has touched 1.7 million people through the organizations he has served. Jason brings rare depth to the intersection of culture strategy and practical leadership development — translating complex organizational challenges into clear, executable frameworks.

He is a certified John Maxwell Team Coach, Speaker, and Trainer — equipped with the tools of one of the world's foremost leadership authorities, and experienced enough to know that tools alone don't change culture. People do.

Jason founded Elevare to bring the kind of culture-building work that was once reserved for Fortune 500 organizations to every leader willing to do the hard work of growing themselves and their teams.

Philosophy

Three pillars, one direction.

PILLAR ONE

Culture is a daily practice

Culture isn't what you put on a wall — it's what you reinforce in every conversation, every decision, every hire. Sustainable culture requires deliberate systems, not aspirational slogans.

PILLAR TWO

Leadership is the lever

The health of any organization traces directly back to the health of its leaders. When leaders grow, teams grow. When teams grow, organizations transform. There is no shortcut around this.

PILLAR THREE

Clarity unlocks performance

Most dysfunction isn't a motivation problem — it's a clarity problem. When people know where they're going, why it matters, and what great looks like, they move. Fast, together, and with ownership.
