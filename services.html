Fractional Engagement

Fractional Chief Culture Officer

Many organizations need senior culture leadership but aren't ready — or don't need — a full-time executive hire. A Fractional CCO gives you the strategic depth and hands-on execution of an experienced culture architect, embedded within your team on a part-time basis.

This is not consulting at arm's length. It's showing up in the room, at the table, with your leaders — week over week — to do the actual work of building a high-performing culture.

How We Work

The Engagement Process

01
Discovery

A focused conversation to understand your organization — where it is, where you want it to be, and what stands between those two realities.

02

03

04
